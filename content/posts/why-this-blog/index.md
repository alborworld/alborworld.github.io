Most of what I learn building platforms ends up in a private repo, a pull
request description, or a conversation that nobody writes down. This is an
attempt to fix that for the parts worth keeping.

What I expect to write about:

- **Platform engineering** — multi-account AWS setups, what "managed entirely as
  code" costs in practice, and where the abstractions leak.
- **CI/CD and governance** — enforcing standards across many repositories
  without turning the pipeline into something people route around.
- **Reliability** — the unglamorous side: backups you have actually restored,
  runbooks somebody other than the author can follow.
- **AI-assisted engineering** — where it genuinely accelerates infrastructure
  work, and the guardrails that make it safe to use on production systems.

Written for the version of me that will hit the same problem again in two years
and not remember the answer.
