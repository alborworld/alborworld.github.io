I design, automate and operate cloud platforms. Twenty-plus years in software
engineering, the last ten of them building and running production cloud
infrastructure across enterprise, financial services and startup environments.

Right now I'm Platform Lead at [Hackurity](https://hackurity.io), where I built
the company's AWS platform from scratch and own its reliability, security and
engineering standards end to end — a multi-account setup managed entirely as
code, containerised services on ECS/Fargate, IaC and CI/CD governance with
automated security scanning, and the disaster-recovery and control work behind
an ISO 27001 audit.

Before that I worked as a freelance platform and DevOps engineer, mostly in
regulated environments: observability and event-driven alerting on EKS at
ABN AMRO, Azure DevOps and Terraform automation at Stater, a Terraform module
ecosystem spanning 50+ projects at LeasePlan, and an AWS platform rebuild at
CarNext. Earlier I led a cross-functional DevOps team at TomTom and spent my
first years as a software engineer — including real-time control software for a
next-generation telescope at the European Southern Observatory.

More recently I've been working on the practical side of AI-assisted
engineering: using it for infrastructure design, automation and documentation
while keeping governance guardrails and mandatory human review on anything that
reaches production. That's a good part of what I expect to write about here.
